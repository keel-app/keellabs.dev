Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026 · Applies to the Lumigate app on iOS and Android

Summary

  • Lumigate needs no account, and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, chips) is stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads. Personalized ads are used only with your consent.
  • You can change your ad choices at any time in your device settings.

We run no server of our own that receives your data, we do not sell data, and we use no analytics tools beyond the services listed above.

02Advertising

Lumigate is free and shows two kinds of Google AdMob ads:

  • Rewarded ads: shown only when you choose to watch one (for example to preview the beam path, pin a prism, or triple a reward).
  • Interstitial ads: shown after you complete a level.

Google may use cookies and advertising identifiers to show relevant ads. See how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

03Your choices

  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch the app asks whether you agree to personalized ads. If you decline, you can still play and will see non-personalized ads.
  • iOS: the app asks for tracking permission through App Tracking Transparency. Change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking or Settings › Apps › Lumigate.
  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads (or Privacy › Ads, depending on the device).

04Children

Lumigate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, contact us and we will address it.

05Retention and deletion

Game progress lives on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID as described in section 03 to unlink it from earlier data.

06Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA or Vietnam's Decree 13/2023/ND-CP), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data, withdraw consent, and object to processing for advertising. Because we keep no personal data on a server, most of these rights are exercised directly through your device and Google settings described in section 03.

07Changes and contact

When this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date at the top.

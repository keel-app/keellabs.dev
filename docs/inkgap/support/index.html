Support

Inkgap is a puzzle game by Keel Labs. Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome at support@keellabs.dev.

01

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your solved levels and your sound and vibration settings are saved on your device only. There is no account and nothing is synced, so deleting the app or switching phones starts you from level 1.

02

I am stuck on a level

Rotate a piece by tapping it; a piece only locks in when it is turned the right way and placed on its spot. Pieces that look identical, such as the two eyes of the owl, can go in either spot. Hints are available from the game screen.

03

How do hints and ads work?

Inkgap is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad after every second solved level, and optional ads you can watch to unlock a hint. If ads are not loading, check your connection and try again later.

04

Can I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), Settings shows a “Privacy choices” row that reopens the consent form. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

05

Something is broken or looks wrong

Email us the device model, the OS version and what happened. A screenshot helps. We read every message.
